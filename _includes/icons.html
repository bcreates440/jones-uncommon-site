{%- comment -%} The icon set, as one inline SVG sprite. Included once per page by _layouts/page.html, then referenced anywhere with: Why inline rather than image files: no extra requests, nothing to go missing, and the shapes take their colour from css/styles.css - so they rebrand with the rest of the site instead of being stranded in the old palette. There are no colours in this file on purpose; `.icon` in the stylesheet supplies the stroke from a token. To add one: copy a , give it an id starting `i-`, keep the 24x24 viewBox, and draw with strokes only (no fills). {%- endcomment -%}
